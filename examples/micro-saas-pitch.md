# Example Pitch: AI WhatsApp Marketing Bot for Indian D2C Brands

### Step 1: The Founder's Pitch
1. **What I sell**: An AI-powered WhatsApp automation bot that recovers abandoned checkouts for Indian Shopify/WooCommerce stores in Hinglish.
2. **Who it's for**: Indian D2C brand owners and Shopify merchants doing ₹5L - ₹50L/month revenue in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities.
3. **Price & Unit Economics**: ₹2,999/month SaaS subscription + ₹0.40 per conversational message (Meta API cost is ₹0.28, giving 30% margin on usage). Landed server compute cost is ~₹150/store. Gross margin is ~85%.
4. **How I'll get first customers**: Direct outbound DMs to 200 Indian Shopify founders on Twitter/LinkedIn with a personalized loom video showing abandoned carts recovered on their own store.
5. **The Ask**: ₹25 Lakh for 10% equity (Implied Valuation: ₹2.5 Crore).

---

### Step 2: The Grilling
1. **💰 TIJORI**: "Meta charges for conversation windows and can increase WhatsApp Business API pricing arbitrarily. If Meta bumps prices by 20% or your customer churns after 2 months because Indian customers find automated WhatsApp messages spammy, what does your lifetime value look like?"
2. **🛒 BAZAAR**: "Every D2C store is already bombarded by Wati, Interakt, QuickSend, and LimeChat. When you DM 200 founders on LinkedIn, why should a founder trust their critical customer phone list to an unproven 1-person tool?"
3. **🧮 HISAAB**: "At ₹3,000/month, you need 84 paying stores just to clear ₹2.5 Lakh/month in base revenue before salaries, server bills, and customer support. How many stores cancel in month 2, and what is your Net Dollar Retention?"
4. **⚔️ TAKKAR**: "Wati has hundreds of crores in funding. LimeChat has an entire enterprise sales team. What stops Interakt or Shopify themselves from releasing this exact Hinglish template tomorrow morning?"
5. **👑 DADA**: "Are you a solo developer or do you have someone who can actually sell to Indian D2C founders? If you spend all day writing code, who handles WhatsApp API verification rejections for your clients?"

---

### Step 3: Verdicts & Offers
- **TIJORI**: "I like the high gross margins, but you have no pricing power against Meta's API tax. For that reason, I'm out."
- **BAZAAR**: "Wati and Interakt already own the app store listings on Shopify. Outbound DMs won't scale. For that reason, I'm out."
- **HISAAB**: "₹2.5 Crore valuation with zero paying customers and unproven churn is daydreaming. For that reason, I'm out."
- **TAKKAR**: "Zero moat. It's a prompt wrapped on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API. For that reason, I'm out."
- **DADA**: "I like that you're a developer who understands unit costs, but you have zero distribution. However, if you pivot to a pure revenue-share model (take 10% of recovered cart value rather than ₹2,999 flat), I'm interested. **MY OFFER**: ₹25 Lakh for 20% equity, conditional on you getting 10 live brand pilots this month."

---

### Step 4: The Scoreboard
- **DEAL METER**: [ 1 / 5 Sharks In ]
- **GREATEST STRENGTH**: Clear, high gross margins on SaaS software and high intent problem (abandoned carts).
- **FATAL FLAW**: Commodity wrapper with high platform dependency on Meta and entrenched competitors (Wati, Interakt).

---

### Step 5: The 7-Day Fix List
1. **Pivoting to Performance-Based Pricing**: Switch from ₹2,999/month to "Free to install + 5% of recovered revenue". Lowers trial barrier to zero.
2. **The 7-Day Zero-Capital Test**: Run manual recovery messages for 3 friendly D2C brands via WhatsApp Business App for 48 hours.
3. **Proof of Success**: If you recover at least ₹15,000 in lost carts within 48 hours, merchants will happily grant API access and refer others.
